Haribo · Halloween digital campaign

Context: Limited Halloween edition
Distribution: Social media

For the release of limited-edition packs, Haribo wanted two films starring its characters: sweets brought to life that transform into Halloween versions of themselves.

Produced entirely in 3D, the two films speak to the brand's consumer community on social media. They were part of a wider digital and on-the-ground campaign run for Halloween.

Storytelling / 3D character animation / Visual effects / Compositing / Editing / Audio editing
