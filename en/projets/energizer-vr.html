Energizer · Pro Series VR experience

Context: Range launch
Distribution: Trade shows, training, client meetings

A virtual reality application supporting the launch of the Pro Series flashlight range, designed to bring the brand's trade show booths to life. The idea: drop users onto a work site and give them three missions in which they choose the best-suited lamp for each situation while exploring its lighting modes.

Built with Unity for standalone headsets, the experience gives free access to the missions from a welcome area, with ergonomics designed to move visitors through quickly. Available in several languages, it is used across European markets, for training as well as client meetings.

Concept / Scriptwriting / 3D modeling / Real-time development / Interface design / Localization
