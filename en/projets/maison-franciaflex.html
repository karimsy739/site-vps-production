Franciaflex · “Maison Franciaflex” virtual tour

Context: Range showcase
Distribution: Online, consumers

An online virtual tour created for Franciaflex, a French manufacturer of windows, doors and indoor and outdoor blinds. The idea: an idealized house where visitors wander freely and discover the products staged in real-life settings.

The environments, created entirely in 3D, are rendered as 360° panoramas. Along the way, each product opens a presentation video and a selection of complementary products. The application is available online and designed for homeowners.

Concept / 3D modeling / 360° rendering / Product videos / Web development
