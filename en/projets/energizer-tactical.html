Energizer · Tactical range

Context: Range relaunch
Distribution: Digital, retailer websites in 10 European countries

With two new products joining the line, Energizer set out to relaunch its Tactical range, designed for private security professionals, law enforcement and tactical units, with a video campaign.

The campaign comprises three films produced entirely in 3D: one per innovation, showcasing its features and uses, followed by a range video bringing all the products together to create a series effect. The films run notably on retailer websites in 10 European countries.
