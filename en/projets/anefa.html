ANEFA · David's story

Context: Promoting jobs in farming
Distribution: Online and at job-seeker events

A film made for ANEFA, an association that promotes careers and jobs in farming. It follows David, a former heating technician looking for work, whose profile ANEFA passed on to Xavier, a poultry farmer who had been searching for a co-worker for years. The introduction led to a stable job for David, and allowed Xavier to hire an unconventional profile who exceeded his expectations.

Shot mainly in live action, the film is shown online and screened at meetings with job seekers.
